I distinctly remember the day when I was introduced to a person that would be perhaps the single greatest career challenge I had.

I had been the manager of a team of data science and ML engineers for a few months, and the boss called us out to some unpleasant mid-West city as it was both the home of this creature, and half way between our New York base and San Francisco. 

"I think we should really move Bob's team under Chase" - that was literally the first words I'd overheard this person saying in a work environment. He was less than a week into the role. He knew neither Bob nor Chase, but that didn't stop him making a recommendation that might affect a few dozen people. A whim, something to say as one does watching a ballgame, a merely trival sentence uttered without thought - but this sentence impacts real lives.
